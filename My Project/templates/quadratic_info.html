Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation is of the form: ax² + bx + c = 0

Key features:

  • Parabola shape
  • Vertex: the highest or lowest point
  • Axis of symmetry: a vertical line through the vertex
  • Roots: where the equation equals zero

Quadratic Formula: x = [-b ± sqrt(b² - 4ac)] / (2a)
